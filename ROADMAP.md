# Roadmap

Ideas we plan to build later. Each one keeps the rule every Toneflix photobook follows: a page should look and feel like printed paper, never like a solid object stuck onto it.

## Book pages

- **Custom frames.** Let creators make their own frames beyond the built-in set: pick the band colour or pattern, mat width and rule weight, or upload their own frame artwork. Uploaded artwork is printed into the page like the built-in frames (translucent ink under the paper grain, no shadows or raised edges), and creators can save frames to reuse across their books and spaces.
- **Shaped photo windows.** Circles, hearts and arches as cut-outs, alongside the current oval.
- **Wheel collage layout.** A round page layout with one photo in the centre and others arranged around it in segments.
